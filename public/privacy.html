Privacy Policy

SN AXIS is designed to work entirely on your device. You do not need an account, and we do not run advertising or third-party tracking.

What the app does

The app connects to your AXIS hardware — the shifter knob, the gauge, and the OBD / CAN module — over Bluetooth. Vehicle information such as speed, RPM, temperatures, battery state and stored fault codes is read from your car and shown to you on your phone. This data stays on your device.

What we never collect

The app never reads, stores, or transmits your VIN or your GPS / location. We do not build a profile of you, and we do not sell any data.

Bluetooth

Bluetooth is used only to communicate with your AXIS hardware. It is not used to track your location or to identify nearby people or devices.

Firmware updates

When you choose to update device firmware, the app downloads the firmware file from a public content-delivery network (jsDelivr / GitHub). These requests carry no personal information about you.

Optional community features

If a community or leaderboard feature is offered and you choose to use it, only the information you explicitly submit (for example a performance time and a car model) is shared. Your VIN and location are never included.

Children

The app is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect information from them.
